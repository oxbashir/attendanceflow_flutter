Pro now follows you. Sign in with Google when you upgrade so your calendars back up and Pro restores if you change phones. Settings moved out of the sidebar — calendars stay up front, files, appearance, and account live in one place. Tighter calendar grid, cleaner add-calendar control, and a gold Pro upgrade card.

What’s new
• Google account for Pro: sign-in, backup, restore, manage or delete
• Settings screen (theme, export, backup, updates)
• Slimmer sidebar: calendars + Upgrade to Pro + Settings
• Tighter day cells and matching calendar-tile borders
